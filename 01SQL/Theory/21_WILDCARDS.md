# Wildcards

## What are Wildcards?

Special characters used with LIKE.

---

## %

Represents zero or more characters.

Example:

LIKE 'R%'

Matches:

Ram
Rohit
Raj

---

## _

Represents exactly one character.

Example:

LIKE '_am'

Matches:

Ram

Not:

Shyam
