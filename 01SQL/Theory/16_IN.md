# IN

## What is IN?

IN allows checking whether a value exists within a list of values.

The IN operator functions as a shorthand for multiple OR conditions, making queries shorter and more readable.

---

## Syntax

SELECT *
FROM employees
WHERE department IN ('IT','HR','Sales');

---

## Why Use IN?

Instead of:

SELECT *
FROM employees
WHERE department='IT'
OR department='HR'
OR department='Sales';

Use:

SELECT *
FROM employees
WHERE department IN ('IT','HR','Sales');

---

## Business Use Cases

- Multiple cities
- Multiple departments
- Multiple product categories

---

## Interview Questions

Q1. IN vs OR?

Q2. Can IN use subqueries?

Answer: Yes

SELECT *
FROM employees
WHERE department_id IN
(
SELECT department_id
FROM departments
);
