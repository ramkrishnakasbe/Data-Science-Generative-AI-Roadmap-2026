# Tables

## What is a Table?

A table is the basic storage structure in a relational database.

Data is organized into rows and columns.

---

## Example

Employees

| Employee_ID | Name | Salary |
| ----------- | ---- | ------ |
| 1           | Ram  | 50000  |
| 2           | Amit | 60000  |

---

## Why Tables?

Tables provide:

* Structured storage
* Easy querying
* Relationship management

---

## Real World Example

Retail System:

Tables:

* Customers
* Orders
* Products
* Payments

---

## Interview Questions

### What is a table?

A collection of related data organized into rows and columns.

### Can tables exist without rows?

Yes.

An empty table can exist.
